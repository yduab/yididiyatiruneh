Explainable AI for Model Interpretability

Independent project

Compared SHAP and LIME explanations across six classifiers — from logistic regression to an RBF SVM — on a medical diagnosis dataset, to measure the accuracy/interpretability trade-off rather than just assert it. The SVM matched logistic regression's accuracy exactly, but its explanations took roughly 200× longer to compute.
